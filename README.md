# Watchmen Micro-Learning

An interactive, sourced micro-learning tool for *Watchmen* by Alan Moore, Dave Gibbons, and John Higgins (DC Comics, 12 issues, 1986–1987).

**Live site:** https://haticekbryldz.github.io/watchmen/

**Languages:** English (default) and Türkçe — use the EN / TR toggle in the site header to switch the full site, including lessons, chapters, quiz, and games.

## What's inside

- **The Basics** — what Watchmen is, its creators, and why it matters
- **Characteristics & Craft** — the nine-panel grid, "Fearful Symmetry," colour, recurring motifs, and the Charlton Comics origins
- **Background** — timeline from the Minutemen to the Keene Act (1977) to October 1985
- **Characters** — lessons on Rorschach, Dr. Manhattan, Nite Owl II, Silk Spectre II, Ozymandias, and The Comedian, plus the Minutemen
- **The Complete Story** — a full summary with a tap-to-reveal, spoiler-gated ending
- **All 12 Chapters** — expandable micro-lessons with real chapter titles and dates, plus a progress tracker
- **Reading Room** — curated articles about Watchmen, with sources cited on the site
- **Watch** — embedded YouTube video essays on the comic
- **Activities** — interactive Doomsday Clock, tappable nine-panel grid, Rorschach inkblot, Match-the-Mask game, and a scored quiz

All chapter summaries and explanations are original writing for education. Sources are cited in the site footer.
